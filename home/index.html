How it works

FOR BUSINESSES RECEIVING
MONTHLY PAYMENTS

Create an account for your business

To start Receiving Payments
You will need to create a client Account for your customers on C-zWena.
that will be linked to your businesses.

Client Account C-zWena.

Once client account links to your business account

Clients will automatically receive payment reminders on Whatsapp
with a link that will take them to they profiles on C-zWena.
Cash users can easily pay you with a OTT Voucher or banking app.

After payment is complete

A receipt will be generated and send to client on Whatapp.Business can view the payment history of each client on their C-zWena account.

More about us

C-zawena is a fintech company in South Africa based in Pietermaritzburg. Our purpose is to help businesses receive online payments from clients who still rely on cash without a need for them to have a bank account.For each online payment made, a digital receipt is generated and sent to the client on WhatsApp.
The businesess will receive a notification of the payment made and the client payment history on the C-zWena platform.
