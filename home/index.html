C-Zawena

Helps Businesses that recieve
monthly payments
from clients who still rely on cash

They can pay from a mobile phone
by converting cash to OTT Voucher
or use online banking.
A digital receipt is generated for each transaction.

How it works

FOR BUSINESSES RECEIVING
MONTHLY PAYMENTS

Create an account for your business

To start Receiving Payments
Create a client Account for your customers with C-Zawena
that will be linked to your businesses Account on C-z.

Once Client Creates an Account

They can then make payments to your business account and
will receive monthly reminders on whatsApp to pay Amount due.

Clients can easily pay you on C-z by logging in to they profiles
Choose between paying with a OTT Voucher or banking App.
After payment is complete,
a receipt will be generated and send to client on Whatapp.
