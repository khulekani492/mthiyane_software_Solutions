Simple, convenient Payments for Your Business.

C-Zawena is an online payment platform that helps businesses collect monthly payments from customers who prefer to pay
using cash vouchers .

GET STARTED

Customers can make payments easily from their phones using a Voucher PIN.

no need to travel or stand in long queues.

Proof of Payment

After every successful payment, a digital receipt is automatically generated and sent to the customer via WhatsApp.

Businesses can also view their customers’ payment history through their C-Zawena dashboard.

How C-Zawena WORKS FOR BUSINESSES

  1. Create Your Business Account

    2. Register your business on C-Zawena and set up your account.

    Businesses Account

    CREATE NOW!

  2. Add Your Customers

    3. Create customer accounts from your C-Zawena business dashboard.

  3. Send Payment Reminders

    4. Customers receive a monthly payment reminder on WhatsApp with a link to the C-Zawena payment page.

    FOR CUSTOMERS

  4. Make a Payment

    5. Customers open the payment link and enter their Voucher PIN to make their payment

  5. Receive a Receipt

    6. Once the payment is successful, a digital receipt is automatically sent to the customer via WhatsApp.
