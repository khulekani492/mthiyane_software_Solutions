More about us

C-zawena is a fintech company in South Africa based in Pietermaritzburg. Our purpose is to help businesses receive online payments from clients who still rely on cash without a need for them to have a bank account.

For each online payment made, a digital receipt is generated and sent to the client on WhatsApp.


The businesess will receive a notification of the payment made and the client payment history on the C-zWena platform.
