How C-Zawena WORKS FOR BUSINESSES

  1. Create Your Business Account

    Register your business on C-Zawena and set up your account.

    CREATE NOW! Business Account

  2. Add Your Customers

    Create customer accounts from your C-Zawena business dashboard.

  3. Register Customers on C-Zawena

    To allow customers to start paying your business with vouchers, a form of consent is generated and sent to the customer’s WhatsApp number when you press the "Add New Customer" button.

    Once the customer completes their registration on C-Zawena, they can immediately start paying using vouchers and will receive automated proof of payment via WhatsApp.

    4. FOR CUSTOMERS

    1. Send Payment Reminders

      Customers receive a monthly payment reminder on WhatsApp with a link to the C-Zawena payment page.

    2. Make a Payment

      Customers open the payment link and enter their Voucher PIN to make their payment.

    3. Receive a Receipt

      Once the payment is successful, a digital receipt is automatically sent to the customer via WhatsApp.

For customers to receive a receipt page, purchase a Digital Receipt Book on your C-Zawena business dashboard.

Starting from R50 for 70 pages.

The price depends on the number of pages, giving your business the option to buy a digital receipt book for they size.

For each payment, one receipt page is deducted from your Digital Receipt Book balance.
