More about us

C-zawena is a fintech company in South Africa based in Pietermaritzburg. Our purpose is to help businesses receive online payments from clients who still rely on cash without a need for them to have a bank account.

We provide a platform for businesses to receive online payments from clients who still rely on cash. The clients can pay from their phones by converting money to OTT Voucher. For each online payment made, a digital receipt is generated and sent to the client on WhatsApp.
